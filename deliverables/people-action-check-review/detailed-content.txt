2 Assess

4 Submit to HR
Share the case for HR

WHAT MANAGERS & HR CAN DO
Managers save and resume private checks, add 
documentation, request Word reports and submit to HR.
HR reviews full submitted cases, downloads reports, adds 
documents and uses the Governance Center.
HOW IT RUNS & WHO GOVERNS
Slack → Cloudflare → Supabase
Cloudflare hosts the app; Supabase stores records. Slack 
stores messages and uploaded files.
PRIVATE BY DESIGN Private drafts; HR reviews submitted case details. Access, retention and audit controls are built in.
No generative AI. Wider rollout still needs access, recovery and delivery checks.
