

PEOPLE ACTION CHECK
One-page Slack app overview  •  October 2026
SLACK HR PILOT
What it does
A manager completes a guided check for a people issue, records facts and documentation, and receives a Low, Medium or High Risk result with practical next steps.
What Slack provides
Private App Home, guided Block Kit forms, manager history, company policies, document attachments, HR alerts and a protected HR review queue with response notes.
Where information is saved
The app appears in Slack. Draft checks remain private to the manager. Submitting creates an HR snapshot and private notification. Messages and files also follow the company’s Slack retention settings.
Who can access what?
Managers can run checks, attach records, view history and read policies. Approved HR reviewers can manage policies and review submissions. Everyone must first be a member of the Slack workspace.
